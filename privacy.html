2. Data We Collect

2.1 Data you provide directly

  • Account credentials (email address, display name) when you sign up via Firebase Authentication.
  • Profile data: age, gender (biological sex), weight, height, and activity level — used exclusively to calculate your personalized hydration goal.
  • Hydration logs: beverage type, volume (ml), and timestamp of each drink entry.
  • Notification preferences and wake/sleep schedule.
  • Feedback messages voluntarily submitted through the in-app form.

2.2 Data collected automatically

  • Device type and operating system version (iOS / Android).
  • App version and build number (included in crash reports and feedback).
  • Approximate location (used only to fetch local weather data for Smart Climate when you explicitly enable it; we do not store GPS coordinates).
  • Activity data (activity intensity and calories burned) read from Apple Health or Google Health Connect — only when you explicitly grant Health permissions in your device settings.
  • Anonymous crash diagnostics (stack traces, device state) collected via Firebase Crashlytics when the App unexpectedly closes.

3. Contact Data Processing

When you contact us by email or through the in-app feedback form, we collect and store the content of your message together with the email address you provide (if any).

This data is used solely to process and respond to your inquiry. It will be deleted once the matter is fully resolved, unless we are legally required to retain it longer.

Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — legitimate interest in responding to communications directed at us; Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for requests relating to a contract.

5. Health Data Transparency

SIP does not sell, rent, share, license, or monetize your health or personal data in any way. Health data is used exclusively to power SIP's core functionality.

Health-related information — including body metrics, hydration logs, activity data, and workout history — is used solely to calculate your personalized daily hydration goal and display your personal progress. It is never uploaded to advertising platforms, data brokers, or analytics services, and it is not used to train machine-learning models.

Health data read from Apple Health or Google Health Connect is processed on-device and only transmitted to Firebase Firestore if you are signed in and have enabled cloud sync, in which case it is stored under your authenticated user ID and accessible only by you.

6. Security and International Data Transfers

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect your data:

  • On-device: Data stored by ObjectBox is protected by your device's built-in encryption (iOS Secure Enclave / Android TEE).
  • In transit: All data transmitted to Firebase is encrypted via TLS 1.3.
  • At rest (cloud): Firebase Firestore uses AES-256 encryption at rest.

Firebase (Google LLC) and RevenueCat are based in the USA. Data transfers to these providers are governed by Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the European Commission, ensuring an adequate level of protection for EU users pursuant to Art. 46 GDPR.

7. Data Retention

  • Local data (ObjectBox): Persists until you uninstall the App or use Settings → Delete Account.
  • Cloud data (Firestore): Permanently and immediately deleted when you use the in-app account deletion flow, which requires re-authentication as a security measure.
  • Firebase Auth records: Deleted immediately on account deletion.
  • Crash reports (Crashlytics): Retained for 90 days per Google's policy, then automatically purged.
  • Contact / feedback emails: Deleted once the inquiry is resolved unless legal retention obligations apply.

8. Your Rights as a Data Subject

Under the GDPR (and Ecuador's LOPDP) you have the following rights regarding your personal data. To exercise any of them, contact us at elvisloja@gmail.com. We will respond within 30 days.

  • 1
    Right of Access (Art. 15 GDPR)You may request a copy of all personal data we hold about you.
  • 2
    Right to Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • 3
    Right to Erasure / "Right to be Forgotten" (Art. 17 GDPR)You may request deletion of your data. Exercisable directly in the App via Settings → Delete Account.
  • 4
    Right to Restriction of Processing (Art. 18 GDPR)You may request that we restrict processing while a dispute about accuracy or lawfulness is resolved.
  • 5
    Notification Obligation (Art. 19 GDPR)We will notify all recipients of your data about any rectification, erasure, or restriction, unless this proves impossible or involves disproportionate effort.
  • 6
    Right to Data Portability (Art. 20 GDPR)You may export your hydration history in CSV format via Settings → Export History.
  • 7
    Right to Withdraw Consent (Art. 7(3) GDPR)Where processing is based on consent (e.g. Health permissions, Crashlytics), you may withdraw it at any time without affecting prior lawful processing.
  • 8
    Right to Lodge a Complaint (Art. 77 GDPR)You have the right to file a complaint with the supervisory authority in your EU member state, or with Ecuador's data protection authority (DINARDAP), without prejudice to any other remedy.

9. Right to Object

You have the right to object at any time to the processing of your personal data.

Where we process data based on a balancing of interests (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — e.g. responding to unsolicited contact), you may object by contacting us. We will then cease processing unless we can demonstrate compelling legitimate grounds that override your interests, or the processing is necessary for the establishment, exercise, or defence of legal claims.

Objection to direct marketing: If we ever use your data for direct marketing purposes, you may object at any time and we will immediately stop using your data for that purpose. No justification is required for this type of objection.

10. Children's Privacy
